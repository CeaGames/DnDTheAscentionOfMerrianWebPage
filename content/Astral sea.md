#NaturalFenomenon 
The [[Astral sea]] is streams of time and space materialized into fluid-like substance. It winds paves paths through the galaxy. The sea is one you can Sail on with a various methods of [[Space Travel]].
You can theoretically swim in sea, however if you stay in it too long you might just get lost to time and space itself, scattered, never to be remembered or found again, this is referred to as drowning. Some people that have fallen in where found years later in a completely different location. There has not been any record of someone directly dying because of the sea and [[Space Travel]] has become a lot safer since the days that people constantly fell into the sea. 

The [[Astral sea]] because of this has become the only way of long distance travel between systems. Sense it is so unpredictable only very skilled sailors are allowed to transport people across. This is the case by most systems governed by [[The Brothers of Merrian]] while [[The Dishonest]] has a lot more [[Pirates]] that try to cross without the "proper education". Many systems in [[The Dishonest]] region argue that the sea is for everyone and should not be limited by those who have their "papers". The biggest sailing school in the galaxy is actually set up in the [[Merrian's Grace Haven]] region, it is called [[Ben Ainslie's Sailors]]. It is free of charge however it has a brutal selection process. There are other schools in systems governed by [[The Brothers of Merrian]] and all of these schools you can become a licenced sailor (That includes [[Ben Ainslie's Sailors]]).
