#RuleSystem 
This regime is ruled by the laws of a dead leader. [[The Rebels]] were in support of this regime since the death of [[The Ancient Caretakers]]. However, after the death of [[The Ancient Caretakers]], people felt the fairest way to rule was by voting for new leadership. This brought about hundreds of years of [[Democratic Capitalism]]. After a lot of campaigning and revolutionary speeches, [[The Rebels]] were elected and brought about the [[necromantic communist regime]]. They completely overthrew [[Democratic Capitalism]] and would get rid of anyone who tried to bring it back, because of the wealth inequality and unfairness it brought. They got elected by majority vote 270 Years (estimate) after [[The Merrian Peace Declaration]]. So now they have been ruling for 30 years again. (Meta note: this is important because Jetty's and Claire's character where adults when the communistic party got elected)

They uses the same laws and morals as these people had at the end of their rule. These are laws that cannot be changed. When new laws have to be added [[The Rebels]] get to decide what must happen. This keeps the work and ancient wisdom of [[The Ancient Caretakers]] alive but also keeps this regime moving forward. This regime is also communist, meaning that all industry is strictly government owned and distributed "equally". [[The Ancient Caretakers]] were believed to be almost omnipotent and god-like. And people loved them as their rule brought incredible prosperity and fairness to their systems.
