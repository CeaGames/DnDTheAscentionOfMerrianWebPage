#Merrian/BrotherOfMerrian #PoliticalPower 
(meta note: Named after the Lighthouse of Alexandria)

Light Xandria Of Merrian was the 3rd brother to be born. He always had a special fascination to the stars which he shared with [[Serif Merrian]]. The way the stars move could supposedly guide the way through time. It can tell us what the universe requires us to do. His father, [[Prince 3]], was part of a species called [[Astral Arachnids]]. It is tradition with this species for when a male arachnid becomes a father to give their body to the mother as a gift of trust for new life. And the fathers spirit would supposedly go on to live in the stars. 

After the [[The Merrian Peace Declaration]], Light X. created the [[Astral Monarchy]] system. Before this system his home system ([[Otheca]]) was ruled by 15 [[Astral Arachnids]] that were priests to [[The Church of Stars]]. However the son found out about a lot of corruption in the group of priests and decided to disband it. 
[[Otheca]] used to be home to only the [[Astral Arachnids]] as the soul intelligent species on the planet. They invented space travel a lot faster then other species in the galaxy but never used it to colonize.
