Even though space travel historically has only been possible by going over the [[Astral sea]], various methods have been invented:


| Method           | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (Astral) Sailing | Astral Sailing, often just called sailing is a method where a sailor has a ship with a big sail in the middle. This sail actually does not catch wind but catches energy and magic flowing through the [[Astral sea]]. This sail acts like a net and a conducter for this energy and magic. The magic gets released bag to the bottom of the ship creating a magic tension layer keeping the boat afloat. The energy is various light, heat and kinetic energy fleeing from the [[Astral sea]] and all [[Space Objects]] around it. |
