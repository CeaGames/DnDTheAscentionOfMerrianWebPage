Even though space travel historically has only been possible by going over the [[Astral sea]], various methods have been invented:


| Method             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (Astral) Sailing   | Astral Sailing, often just called sailing is a method where a sailor has a ship with two big sails in the middle. This was originally invented by the [[Astral Arachnids]] as the sails were made from their silk. These sails actually do not catch wind but catches energy and magic flowing through the [[Astral sea]]. It does this by lowering itself below the ship. |
| Organic Propulsion | Organic propulsion was invented originally by the [[Carni's]], specifically the Lion subrace.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
