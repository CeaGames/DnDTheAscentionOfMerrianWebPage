#Merrian #PoliticalPower
Goddess, queen, peacekeeper and so many other titles are given to Serif Merrian. However no one knows what being she actually is. She does not have a physical form, or at least not at this time. People speculate on her coming to the galaxy in disguise to birth [[The Brothers of Merrian|her children]], some others believe she created [[The Brothers of Merrian|her Children]] using the DNA of [[The Prince's of Merrian]].
She brought peace to the galaxy by initiating [[The Merrian Peace Declaration]].

![[PictureWomenOutOfStarsMerrian.png]]

During Merrian's grace period she found [[The Prince's of Merrian|the 7 most powerful men]] in the galaxy and offered them a way to rule in peace. The men agreed on 1 condition, they wanted to have an offspring with Merrian so that her power will be with her next of kin.
Over a span of 35 years every 5 years a festival of fertility would be held where people danced, drank and created offspring themselves. This was very successful because it brought people together and they started treating Merrian as a godlike diplomat. After the [[The Brothers of Merrian| 7 children]] where born consecutively each 5 years of age apart, they waited for [[Olym Zeus of Merrian|the last brother]] to be 20 years old, adulthood for it's species. When this time came to be [[The Merrian Peace Declaration]] was initiated.
