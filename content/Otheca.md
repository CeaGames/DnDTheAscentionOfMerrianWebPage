#Location 
(meta note: named after the scientific name for silken cocoon/egg sac/"spider's nest")

[[Otheca]] was ruled by 15 [[Astral Arachnids]] that were priests to [[The Church of Stars]]. However the [[Light Xandria Of Merrian]] found out about a lot of corruption in the group of priests and decided to disband it. 
[[Otheca]] used to be home to only the [[Astral Arachnids]] as the soul intelligent species on the planet. They invented space travel a lot faster then other species in the galaxy but never used it to colonize. It was always in service of getting closer to the stars, reading them better and serving their will. 
However, after [[The Merrian Peace Declaration]] Light had to rule over a lot more then just [[Otheca]] and people the arachnids started going to many other planets, and the people from other planets started going to [[Otheca]].
